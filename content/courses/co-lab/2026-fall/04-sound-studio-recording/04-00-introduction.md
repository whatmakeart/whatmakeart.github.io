## Overview

This module introduces podcast production through a short group conversation recorded in the Interactive Media Lab's Recording Studio. Working in small groups, students turn prepared topics into a focused discussion with a clear beginning, middle, and end. Each group will rotate through the roles of host, guest, and producer while learning microphone technique, headphone monitoring, level control, and recording.

The RØDECaster will capture a ready-to-use stereo mix as well as separate microphone tracks. Students will use the stereo mix for a fast transcript-based edit in Adobe Premiere Pro or Adobe Podcasts. The separate tracks provide a backup and can be brought into Adobe Audition when individual voices need more control or repair.

In post-production, students will edit with an emphasis on meaning rather than merely removing every pause. The goal is to tighten the conversation, preserve natural timing, repair distracting audio problems, balance voices, add simple transitions or music when appropriate, and export a clear final podcast.

## Learning Objectives

- Organize a short podcast conversation around a driving question
- Perform the roles of host, guest, and recording producer
- Correctly position a microphone and monitor sound with headphones
- Set and observe safe recording levels without clipping
- Record and preserve both a stereo mix and isolated microphone tracks
- Use transcription and Text-Based Editing in Adobe Premiere Pro to create a rough cut
- Use Adobe Audition when individual microphone tracks require repair or balancing
- Edit speech while preserving clarity, pacing, and the speakers' intended meaning
- Export an archival WAV master and a compressed listening copy
