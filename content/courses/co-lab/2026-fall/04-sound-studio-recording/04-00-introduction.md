## Overview

This module introduces podcast production through a short group conversation recorded in the IML podcast booth. Working in small groups, you will turn one of your prepared topics into a focused discussion with a clear beginning, middle, and end. Each group will rotate through the roles of host, guest, and producer while learning microphone technique, headphone monitoring, level control, and RØDECaster recording.

The RØDECaster will capture a ready-to-use stereo mix as well as separate microphone tracks. You will use the stereo mix for a fast transcript-based edit in Adobe Premiere Pro. The separate tracks provide a backup and can be brought into Adobe Audition when individual voices need more control or repair.

In post-production, you will edit for meaning rather than merely removing every pause. You will tighten the conversation, preserve natural timing, repair distracting audio problems, balance voices, add simple transitions or music when appropriate, and export a clear final podcast.

## Learning Objectives

- organize a short podcast conversation around a driving question
- perform the roles of host, guest, and recording producer
- position a microphone and monitor sound with headphones
- set and observe safe recording levels without clipping
- record and preserve both a stereo mix and isolated microphone tracks
- organize and archive RØDECaster recording files
- use transcription and Text-Based Editing in Adobe Premiere Pro to create a rough cut
- use Adobe Audition when individual microphone tracks require repair or balancing
- edit speech while preserving clarity, pacing, and the speakers' intended meaning
- balance dialogue, music, and sound effects
- export an archival WAV master and a compressed listening copy
