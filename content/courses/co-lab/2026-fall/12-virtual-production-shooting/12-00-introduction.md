## Overview

Use this XR Studio session to record the main actions in your virtual production. Build on your script, storyboard or animatic, and the tests from last class. Continue making props and adjusting the virtual environment, concentrating on what will appear in the camera frame.

Work together to arrange the scene, rehearse an action, record it, and review the result. Check that the subject is visible, important sounds are clear, and the physical props and virtual background support the same idea. The instructor will help with the Unreal, disguise, camera, and lighting setup. You do not need to operate every system independently.

Before next class, begin your own rough cut: an unfinished edit that shows how the shots work together. Follow the [shooting checklist](12-01-studio-shooting-checklist.md) and [rough-cut guide](12-02-build-your-first-rough-cut.md). Review the [final project requirements](../15-virtual-production-final-shooting/15.03-virtual-produciton-final-project-assignment.md) now so you know what you are working toward.

## Learning Objectives

- Coordinate a simple shoot and contribute to shared production work.
- Use framing, physical props, and a virtual background to communicate an idea.
- Record and review useful shots and sound.
- Organize shared footage and begin an individual edit.
- Identify what needs to be recorded or improved next class.

## Key Terms

- **Blocking:** Where the performer, camera, and props are placed and how they move.
- **Take:** One recorded attempt at a shot.
- **Coverage:** Additional views or details that give you choices when editing.
- **Continuity:** Keeping important details consistent between shots.
- **Edit handles:** Extra recorded time before and after the action.
- **Rough cut:** An unfinished edit used to test the sequence and find missing material.
