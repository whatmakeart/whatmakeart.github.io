## Overview

Students use the first stage test to improve the relationship between the performer, physical elements, and virtual environment. The crew matches the direction, color, and apparent brightness of the lighting, while team members rehearse the planned action alongside the camera operator and performers. Reviewers judge the overall result through both the recorded image and the live monitor feed.

The director and crew refine blocking, eyelines, screen direction, focus, and audio levels. Editors test the transitions between shots to ensure the sequence cuts together clearly. Team members adjust only one variable at a time, compare the resulting takes, and document the specific settings and cues that succeed. By the conclusion of rehearsal, the production team establishes a repeatable setup and compiles a focused list of remaining revisions.

## Learning Objectives

- Compare physical and virtual lighting through the camera image
- Adjust light direction and color to support the intended visual relationship
- Recognize distracting exposure, focus, reflection, or display artifacts in a test recording
- Rehearse performer and camera movement within the available stage space
- Plan eyelines, screen direction, and matching actions across shots
- Check dialogue or other recorded sound before shooting the complete sequence
- Record usable settings, cues, and changes so the team can repeat the setup
