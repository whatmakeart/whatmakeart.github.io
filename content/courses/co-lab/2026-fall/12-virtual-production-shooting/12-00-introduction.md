## Overview

Students use the first recordings from the previous [Virtual Production](../../../../video/virtual-production/virtual-production.md) shoot to decide which setup changes will help and which shots they can complete today. Students use their script or action outline, storyboard or animatic, shot list, props, and available digital assets to continue producing and capturing footage in the XR Studio.

Three XR Studio sessions remain, including today. Students use these sessions to build and refine environments, make physical props, record footage, and improve individual edits. Students check the camera image, lighting, background, and sound and record the main actions that are ready and review each take before changing the setup.

Students share production roles and footage. While another group uses the stage, students should develop props, prepare sounds, assist other classmates, or begin their own edits.

## Learning Objectives

- Use the first recordings to improve a scene.
- Coordinate useful production tasks with classmates.
- Record clear actions and views that support the idea.
- Review takes, share media, and maintain a backup.
- Begin making individual decisions about shot selection and timing.

## Key Terms

- **Take:** One recorded attempt at a shot.
- **Coverage:** Additional views or details that provide editing choices.
- **Continuity:** Keeping important details consistent between shots.
- **Shot log:** A brief note identifying a recording and useful takes.
- **Rough cut:** An unfinished edit used to see how the shots work together.
