## Overview

A rough cut helps you discover what your video needs while you still have access to the XR Studio. Use the [Adobe Premiere](../../../../video/adobe-premiere-pro/adobe-premiere.md)d editing skills from the [earlier footage and sound exercise](../03-make-the-illusion/03-06-edit-virtual-production-footage-and-add-sound-effects-assignment.md). Keep the edit simple enough to revise quickly.

## Make Your Own Edit

1. **Organize your files.** Keep your editing project, footage, audio, and exports in clearly labeled folders. Preserve the original recording folders and a backup.
2. **Check playback.** Open a few clips before you begin. If the footage will not play smoothly on your laptop, ask the instructor for smaller editing copies, called proxies, or an available lab computer.
3. **Create your own project or sequence.** Import the shared recordings and your sound. Each student chooses and arranges their own shots.
4. **Assemble the sequence.** Place useful takes in an order that shows a beginning, a change or development, and an ending. Start with simple cuts and trim unnecessary pauses.
5. **Mark missing material.** Use a labeled storyboard image or temporary title such as “Missing close-up of the object.” A rough cut may contain placeholders; the final video should replace them with finished material.
6. **Add enough sound to test the idea.** Place important dialogue, effects, or ambience. Detailed sound cleanup and color adjustments can wait until the sequence works.
7. **Watch without stopping.** What can a viewer understand? Where does the sequence feel rushed, slow, or incomplete? List the most important fixes.

Keep the existing target of approximately 30–60 seconds in mind. Use the strongest material rather than stretching every take.

## Bring to Next Class

Bring your rough cut and the files needed to open it. A short export is useful for quick playback. For [Progress Report 3](12-03-virtual-production-progress-report-3-assignment.md), a timeline screenshot or short export is enough evidence; a finished video is not expected yet.
