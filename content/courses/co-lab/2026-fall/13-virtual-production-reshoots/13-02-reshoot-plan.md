## Record the Most Useful Changes

Choose a few achievable tasks from the in-class peer rough cut review. Continue essential shooting if your first version is still missing major actions. Check new takes while the setup is available, then try them in your own edit when possible.

Before the end of class, make a short plan. List essential tasks first. Save a setup photo if a later shot must match today's work. Confirm that collaborators can access the recordings and that your project has a backup. You may add it to your existing shot list:

<div class="responsive-table-markdown">

| Shot or task                  | Why the edit needs it    | People, props, or setup needed              |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| Close-up of the moving object | Makes the change visible | Object, performer, same lighting            |
| Record footsteps              | Connects two actions     | Performer, microphone, quiet recording time |

</div>
