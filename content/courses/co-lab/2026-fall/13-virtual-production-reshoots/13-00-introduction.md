## Overview

Each student records their individual project sequence using the settings and workflow established during rehearsal. Working collaboratively within their group, students rotate through crew responsibilities while helping each member capture all the views, actions, reactions, and sounds required for their personal vision. Classmates review takes together while the studio setup remains active so the project owner can fix problems immediately during the session.

Every student organizes and backs up their own footage, then assembles an early rough cut. The individual filmmaker analyzes this cut to locate missing information, continuity gaps, pacing errors, or audio flaws, often seeking quick feedback from group partners. Each creator compiles these discoveries into a prioritized pickup list for their next shoot. Evaluating the edit early gives students time to adjust their individual plans while their collaborative group and studio stage remain available.

## Learning Objectives

- Coordinate a shoot using a shot list and clearly assigned crew responsibilities
- Record useful coverage with enough time before and after the action for editing
- Maintain continuity of movement, props, eyelines, and screen direction
- Log takes and identify the strongest usable performances
- Preserve original footage and maintain a backup before editing
- Assemble a rough cut that communicates the intended progression
- Identify missing shots or sounds and prepare a prioritized pickup list
