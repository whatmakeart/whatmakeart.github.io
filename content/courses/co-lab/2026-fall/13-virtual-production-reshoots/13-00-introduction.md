## Overview

Begin by watching your individual rough cut with a classmate. Identify what a viewer can follow and where a shot, sound, or clearer action is missing. Use that feedback to decide what to record today. Continue essential shooting as needed, then reshoot or add details that will help the sequence.

Work together on production while each student develops their own edit. Aim to leave with the main footage for the complete sequence so the final shooting session can focus on a small number of final additions and finishing work. Simplify a scene when that will make the idea clearer and more achievable.

Use the [reshoot plan](./13-02-reshoot-plan.md) during class.

## Learning Objectives

- Use an unfinished edit to identify missing or confusing material.
- Give useful feedback based on what you see and hear.
- Record changes that improve the sequence.
- Coordinate shared production around individual editing needs.
- Leave class with a practical plan for the final studio session.

## Key Terms

- **Rough cut:** An unfinished edit that shows how your shots work together.
- **Pacing:** How shot length, action, and sound affect the rhythm of the video.
- **Pickup shot:** An extra shot that fills a gap or clarifies a detail.
- **Reshoot:** A new recording made to replace an earlier take.
- **Continuity:** Consistency between shots, such as prop position, movement, and lighting.
