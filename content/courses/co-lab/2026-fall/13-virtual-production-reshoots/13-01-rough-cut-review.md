Watch the rough cut once without an explanation of the story.

1. What happens or changes in the video? Which image or sound communicates the idea most clearly?
2. Where do you need another view, a clearer action, a different timing, or a sound to understand the sequence?
3. What is the most useful change the group can record today?
4. What shots look well composed and professional?
5. What shots look unconsidered and amateurish?

Point to a specific moment rather than saying only that you like or dislike the video. For example: “I could not see what the performer picked up. A closer view might help.”

Write down your observations in the discussion reply.
