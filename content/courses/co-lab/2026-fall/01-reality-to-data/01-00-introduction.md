## Overview

Introduction to Co/Lab and the Cleveland Institute of Art's Interactive Media Lab. Students begin in the 3D capture studio with hands on 3D structured light scanning and photogrammetry workflows to capture content from reality and turn it into digital content and assets.

## Learning Objectives

- Capture a head scan with a structured light scanner
- Capture a photogrammetry scan and export a 3D mesh

## Keywords

- 3D Scanning
- Photogrammetry
