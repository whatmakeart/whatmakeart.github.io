## Assignment Deliverables

1. Submit a list of 3 related but different camera shots for virtual production shooting
   - Write a 1–2 sentence description of each camera shot

## Assignment Overview

Review camera [Shot Types](../../../../video/shot-types.md). Remember to change the angle of the camera and the distance from the subject with each new shot.
