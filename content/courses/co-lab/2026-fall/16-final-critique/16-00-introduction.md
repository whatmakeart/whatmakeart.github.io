## Overview

Share your completed individual video and discuss how images, physical and virtual space, action, and sound communicate your idea. Briefly identify your contribution to the shared production and one editing choice that shaped your version.

Give feedback by describing specific moments in the work. Discuss what you understood, which choices were effective, and what could develop further. You can respond to a story, a mood, a visual transformation, or an abstract sequence.

## Learning Objectives

- Present your completed work and explain an intentional choice.
- Identify your contributions and acknowledge collaborators.
- Discuss how framing, editing, and sound affect the viewer's experience.
- Give useful feedback with specific examples.
- Reflect on collaboration and one useful next step for future work.

## Key Terms

- **Critique:** A discussion that helps us understand and develop creative work.
- **Intention:** What the maker wants the work to communicate or explore.
- **Audience:** The people experiencing the work.
- **Attribution:** Giving credit for another person's contribution or material.
- **Reflection:** Looking back at a process to identify what you learned.
