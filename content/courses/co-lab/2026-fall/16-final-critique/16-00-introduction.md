## Overview

Each student presents their completed virtual production project to the class and discusses how image, performance, space, and sound work together to communicate the concept. The creator explains the intention behind their piece, details the group's collaborative shooting process, highlights their own contributions, and describes the revisions that shaped the final video.

Peers reference specific timestamps from each video to provide constructive feedback on framing, pacing, continuity, audio design, and the seamless integration of physical and virtual elements. During the critique, students reflect on their crew communication, rotating studio responsibilities, and technical problem-solving alongside the aesthetic qualities of the finished work. Finally, each creator identifies what adjustments they would make in a future production and selects key documentation—such as behind-the-scenes records, setup diagrams, and reference stills—that clearly represents their complete process.

## Learning Objectives

- Present a completed video with a clear explanation of its intention and process
- Identify personal contributions and acknowledge collaborators and source assets
- Analyze how framing, editing, performance, and sound affect the viewer's experience
- Support critique comments with specific observations from the work
- Evaluate how planning, communication, and shared responsibilities affected production
- Identify useful technical and creative revisions for future projects
- Select documentation and preserve files that support later presentation or portfolio use
