## Assignment Overview

Spend approximately four hours outside class advancing your virtual production and preparing for the third XR Studio session. Use footage and tests to decide what to revise, record, or simplify. Keep reporting brief so most of your time goes into production.

Continue collaborating on production and sharing media. Write your own report and begin your own edit. Credit shared work and identify your personal contribution.

### Work Completed and Next Steps

- What did you personally complete since [Report 1](../10-props-costumes-and-3d-environments/10-04-virtual-production-progress-report-1-assignment.md)? Briefly list activities and approximate outside-class time.
- Show new or revised work. Explain what improved, what a recording or early edit revealed, and what remains unresolved.
- Identify three priority tasks for capturing the footage and sound your edit needs. List studio requirements, collaborators, assistance, and work to complete elsewhere.

Include relevant clips, sketches, references, prop progress, environment tests, screenshots, timeline views, or XR Studio photos. Label the evidence, credit collaborators and sources, and check access to linked media.
