title: 03.03 Listen and Evaluate Sounds
date: 2026-09-09T12:00:00-04:00
lastmod: 2026-09-09T11:53:07-04:00
---

After preserving your original audio file and making an archival lossless `WAV` backup file, listen to the audio. Think about what it sounds like.

Is there a lot of hiss?
Is it full of static?
Is it quiet?
Is it loud?
Are there people talking in the background?
What about long bangs or other unwanted noises?
Is there a spot where the wind picks up?
Does the recording pickup where you are handling the phone?

Listen to the audio multiple times at multiple volume levels, ideally wth headphones. _Never listen too loud_

Write down a list of problems with the audio. Try to be as descriptive and specific as possible. This will be your roadmap for cleaning up and leveling the audio file.
