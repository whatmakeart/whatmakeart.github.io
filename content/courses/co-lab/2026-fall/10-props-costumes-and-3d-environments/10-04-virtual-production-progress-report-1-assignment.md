1. Brief individual progress report with labeled images and/or links to video or audio documentation. Concise bullet points are welcome.
   - Label file YYYY-MM-DD Lastname Firstname Virtual Production Progress Report 1 (`.pdf`, `.docx`).

## Assignment Overview

Spend approximately 3.5 - 4 hours outside class developing your [virtual production](../../../../video/virtual-production/virtual-production.md) and preparing for the second of four XR Studio sessions. Put your time into the project; keep the report brief.

Collaborate on scripts, props, environments, performances, shooting, and other production work. Write and submit your own report.Identify your contribution to shared work. Each student will also create their own final edit from the available footage.

### Work Completed and Next Steps

- What did you personally complete in class and outside class? Briefly list activities and approximate outside-class time.
- Include labeled documentation of progress. Explain what a test revealed, what changed, and what still needs improvement.
- Identify three priority tasks. Separate work that needs the XR Studio from work you can complete elsewhere. List equipment, collaborators, or assistance needed.

Documentation may include recorded clips, references, sketches, prop construction, environment tests, screenshots of recordings or timelines, and XR Studio photos. Explain how references informed work you completed. Credit collaborators, references, tutorials, and downloaded assets. Check that linked media opens for the instructor.
