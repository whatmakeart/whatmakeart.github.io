## Assignment Deliverables

Submit a brief Canvas text entry with at least one starting point for a scene in your virtual production. Choose any of these options:

- Link to a 3D environment from [Fab](https://www.fab.com/) or [BlenderKit](https://www.blenderkit.com/).
- Submit links to individual 3D assets or asset packs you could combine into an environment, such as rocks, industrial equipment, traffic cones, furniture, or other appropriate objects.
- If you cannot find a suitable environment, briefly describe the setting you want to create.

Identify which scene the environment or assets would support. You may include options for additional scenes, but only one initial environment idea is needed. Links or a short description are enough. You do not need to download assets or build the environment for this assignment.

## Assignment Overview

Over the next four class sessions, we will build 3D environments, make physical props, and shoot virtual productions. An initial selection helps the instructor prepare the Unreal scenes and disguise setup before class so some shots can begin at the start of the session.

_Submit earlier if possible to allow more preparation time._

There is **no penalty for submitting by the start of class**. Your selection is a starting point and may change as the project develops.
