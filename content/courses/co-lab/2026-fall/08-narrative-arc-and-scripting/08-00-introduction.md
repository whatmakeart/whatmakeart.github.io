### 5. Connect the Analysis to Virtual Production

Reflect briefly on how you might use these ideas in your own virtual production work.

Consider how you would reproduce one or more of these compositions in the XR Studio.

What would you need to control?

- Camera position, distance, and height
- Actor or object placement
- Foreground and background relationships
- Virtual environment composition
- Camera movement and available recording space

Think about which shots would be easy to reproduce and which might require additional planning.
