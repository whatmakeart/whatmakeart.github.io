## Overview

Move the bicycle sculpture from an active construction project toward a completed work that can be presented and activated. Prioritize a stable structure, a functioning kinetic element, resolved connections, and an intentional visual finish.

Completion also includes a clear operating procedure and useful documentation. Review the project with the instructor before a live demonstration and make the final changes needed for the bicycle presentation.

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguish essential completion tasks from optional additions.
- Demonstrate an intentional moving element using bicycle components.
- Inspect joints, fasteners, exposed edges, and moving clearances.
- Resolve surface treatment in relation to the work's intent.

## Key Terms

- Punch list: A short list of corrections needed before completion.
- Activation: The action that causes or begins the artwork's movement.
- Pinch point: A location where moving parts can trap a body part.
- Finish: The final treatment and appearance of a surface.
- Operating procedure: Instructions for setting up, activating, and stopping the work.
