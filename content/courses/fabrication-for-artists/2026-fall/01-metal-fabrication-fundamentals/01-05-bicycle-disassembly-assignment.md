Rather than designing a conventional bicycle, consider the existing frame and components as raw material. Wheels can become rotating sculptural elements. Pedals can activate mechanisms. Frames can be stretched, stacked, widened, shortened, or combined. Handlebars, forks, cranks, chains, sprockets, and other components can be reassigned new structural or kinetic functions.
