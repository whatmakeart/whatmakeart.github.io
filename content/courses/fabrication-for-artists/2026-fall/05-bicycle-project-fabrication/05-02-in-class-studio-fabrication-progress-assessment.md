## End of Class Goal Reflection

At the end of class, return to your original Canvas discussion post and review the goals that you wrote at the beginning of class. Submit a reply to this new discussion thread.

For each goal, indicate whether it was:

- Completed
- Partially completed
- Not completed

Briefly explain what you accomplished, any problems or unexpected discoveries you encountered, and what you need to work on next.

Conclude your reflection by identifying 1–3 specific next steps for your project. Remember, it is best to write each new step or goal as a description of what it will look like when the goal is completed.

For example:

- Finish welding the wheel support so the wheel can rotate freely without contacting the frame.
- Test the crank mechanism and identify any locations where it binds or needs adjustment.
- Cut and fit the remaining tubing for the upper structure so it is ready to tack weld.

These should help you begin your next outside of class studio session with a clear plan rather than spending valuable time in the studio deciding what to do next.
