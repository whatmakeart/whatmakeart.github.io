## In-Class Work Plan

Before beginning work today, identify **3–5 specific goals** you plan to complete on your kinetic bicycle sculpture by the end of class.

Your goals should be concrete and achievable during today’s studio time. They might include cutting or fitting tubing, welding a particular section, testing a moving component, refining a mechanism, cleaning or preparing metal, modeling a 3D print, or solving a specific fabrication problem.

You can also list questions that you may have or problems that you encountered since next class and need to solve.

## Submission

Reply to the Canvas discussion thread with a numbered list of your 3–5 goals for today. Add brief descriptions to what the goals are. Its best tro write the goals as a description of what it would look like to have the goal completed.

At the end of class, be prepared to briefly evaluate which goals you completed and what your next steps will be.
