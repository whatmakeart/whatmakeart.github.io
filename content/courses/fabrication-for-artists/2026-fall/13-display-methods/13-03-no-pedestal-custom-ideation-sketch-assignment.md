## Assignment Overview

Choose an existing artwork, an artwork you are developing, or an object that stands in for one. Design a simple, custom display device for a specific three-dimensional artwork or part of a larger artwork. Move beyond the conventional pedestal. Consider how a support, fixture, holder, or framework can become an intentional aesthetic part of presenting the work. Create Exploratory sketches from 45–90 minutes of active in-class ideation, showing at least three distinct approaches. Keep the design simple enough to fabricate while making its relationship to the artwork specific and intentional.

Create a developed sketch of your preferred concept showing:

- The artwork and its custom display device. _Can be a simple approximation_
- Approximate dimensions, viewing height, and orientation.
- Proposed materials, fabrication techniques, and attachment method.
- A human figure for scale and audience viewing position. _Can be a simple stick figure_

Possible directions include:

- A steel rod welded to a base plate with a custom fixture supporting the artwork.
- A 3D-printed or laser-cut holder shaped for a particular object.
- A bent or bonded acrylic support.
- A custom speaker holder for an installation or an adjustable projector holder for projection mapping.
- A steel or acrylic mannequin-like framework for clothing, or a smaller support for jewelry and accessories.
- A combination of materials and processes.

## Sketching Process

Begin with quick, varied possibilities before developing one concept.

Consider:

- How does the device direct attention toward the artwork?
- What do its material, shape, and visible connections communicate?
- How do height, angle, distance, and surrounding space affect viewing?
- Can people of different heights or seated positions experience the work?
- How will the artwork attach securely, be removed, and avoid damage?
- Should the support be visually prominent, discreet, or integrated into the artwork?
