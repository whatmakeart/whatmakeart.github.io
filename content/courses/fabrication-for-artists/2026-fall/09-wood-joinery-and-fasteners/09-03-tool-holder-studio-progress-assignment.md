## Assignment Deliverables

1. ~4 Hours of Work on Tool Holder Project
   - Complete approximately 4 hours of focused work outside of scheduled class time
2. Progress Documentation
   - At least 1 image documenting meaningful progress.
   - Label file(s) YYYY-MM-DD Lastname Firstname Tool HolderProgress (`.jpg`, `.png`, `.pdf`)
3. Brief Progress Note
   - Label file YYYY-MM-DD Lastname Firstname Tool Holder Progress Note (`.docx`, `.pdf`)

## Assignment Overview

### Studio Work

Review the [tool holder design requirements](../08-wood-fabrication-introduction/08-03-tool-holder-ideation-assignment.md).

Spend approximately 4 hours making meaningful progress on your tool holder project. Your work will depend on the direction of your individual project. You are not expected to complete the holder by nest class.

Mistakes, failed tests, and revisions can be meaningful project progress when they help you understand how the project needs to develop.

### Progress Report

- What you worked on and your progress
- What went well or what did not work
- What you plan to work on next and the steps
- Include images from multiple stages rather than only the final outcome
