## Overview

Students present their custom laser cut display stands and make iterative revisions for rapid testing of new configurations on the laser cutter. Creating 3D forms with interlocking pieces cut on a laser cutter is introduced. Students use the online [MakeSlice Laser Cutter Slicer](../../../../digital-fabrication/laser-cutting/make-slice-laser-cutter-slicer.md) to turn 3D meshes into interlocking or stacking laser-cut objects from cardboard.
