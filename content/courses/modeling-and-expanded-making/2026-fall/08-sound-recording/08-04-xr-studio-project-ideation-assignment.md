## Assignment Deliverables

Submit one PDF containing:

1. Three _unique_ project concepts, each with a title and a 3–5 sentence description explaining what happens and what the audience experiences.
   - For each idea or concept include:
     - One sketch, storyboard, or reference image per concept.
     - A proposed tool list. Identify what will be performed live, generated live, or prepared beforehand.
2. Your preferred concept, with a brief explanation of why you selected it and one small technical test you could conduct first.

## Assignment Overview

Develop a minimum of three unique and different project ideas that explore the creative possibilities of the XR Studio. Projects may include live performance, recorded video, animation, interactive installation, or a combination of physical and digital elements.

Start with an experience, story, or artistic question. Consider how performers, virtual environments, sound, lighting, props, and costumes could work together. Select the tools that support your idea; a successful project can use a simple setup.

### Possible Project Directions

Use these starting points, combine them, or develop your own:

#### Virtual Production Scene

Create a short narrative or music video with performers inside an Unreal Engine environment displayed through disguise. Coordinate camera framing, ETC lighting cues, and lights on stands to connect the physical performers with the virtual setting.

#### Motion Capture Animation

Record [Motion Capture](../../../../3d-modeling/motion-capture.md) movements with OptiTrack and apply them to original characters. Explore how an ordinary action changes meaning when performed by an unexpected character in an unfamiliar environment.

#### Live Motion Capture Performance

Have a performer control a digital character during a live event. Develop a relationship between the physical body and its virtual counterpart through imitation, disagreement, transformation, or choreography.

#### Generative Music Video

Use TouchDesigner to create visuals that respond to music, voices, or movement. Explore recorded or generated audio, rhythmic editing, and coordinated lighting changes.

#### Pose-responsive AI Environment

Propose a workflow using pose detection in TouchDesigner to guide images or video generated with ComfyUI, with RenderStream and disguise as potential display connections. Consider how transformations and processing delays could become part of the performance.

#### Lighting as a Storytelling Tool

Use the ETC lighting board, studio lights, virtual scenery, and sound to tell a story through changing atmosphere. Plan a sequence in which lighting reveals, conceals, or transforms the meaning of an action. Record multiple cues and transitions to pair with live or recorded performance.

#### Hybrid Props and Costumes

Design laser-cut or 3D-printed objects that connect physical performers to a digital world. Consider wearable extensions, miniature sets, masks, or props whose significance changes with the surrounding imagery.

## Five Additional Project Prompts

1. **The Room Remembers:** Record a performer’s actions, then have them interact with traces of their earlier movements. Build a scene around repetition, memory, or an encounter with a past self.

2. **A Collective Creature:** Let several performers control different parts of one virtual creature or environment. Make cooperation—and occasional disagreement—visible to the audience.

3. **Impossible Scale:** Combine a fabricated miniature or oversized prop with virtual scenery to make a performer appear enormous or tiny. Use framing and performance to make the scale change convincing.

4. **Human and Machine Misunderstanding:** Build a performance around a digital system that responds imperfectly to a person. Turn delayed, exaggerated, or unexpected responses into a deliberate comic or dramatic relationship.

5. **One Scene, Three Moods:** Stage the same short action three times, changing the virtual background, lighting, and sound. Explore how those choices transform the audience’s interpretation.
