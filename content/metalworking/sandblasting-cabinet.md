[Remove Paint with Sandblasting Cabinet](https://youtu.be/AdtvlSiGnSg)

A sandblasting cabinet uses compressed air and abrasive media to remove paint, rust, and other coatings from metal. Sandblasting can be relatively slow, so it is often best for smaller areas or detailed cleaning rather than stripping an entire large object.

Sandblasting is especially useful for:

- Irregular shapes
- Corners and joints
- Curved bicycle tubing
- Areas that are difficult to reach with other tools
