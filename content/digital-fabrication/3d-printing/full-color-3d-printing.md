The think\[box\] at Case Western Reserve University offers full color [3D printing services](https://case.edu/thinkbox/equipment/3d-printing/industrial-polyjet-3d-printers) at reasonable prices.

Choose 2 of the following.

- Full Color and Flexible
- Full Color and Rigid
- Flexible and Rigid Combined but Single Color

These print services are by online order.

Use a combination of your 3D Scanned head scans to create wearable body extensions or other forms with full color textures for 3D printing at the think\[box\].
