Panel saws allow for safe cross cutting of large sheet goods such as [plywood](plywood.md). Once panels are cut to smaller sizes further cutting can be done on a [table saw](how-to-use-a-table-saw.md).

Before using a vertical [woodworking](woodworking.md) panel saw, complete shop training and follow the machine’s manual before operating.

1. Wear safety glasses and hearing protection. Tie back long hair, secure loose clothing, and clear the work area.
2. Check that guards are installed, dust collection is connected, and the material fits the saw’s capacity. Check for nails and screws.
3. Place the panel on the rollers against the frame supports. Support the sheet and offcut. Align your measurement, allowing for the blade’s cutting width.
4. Disconnect power before adjustments. For vertical cuts, start with the carriage at the top. For horizontal cuts, rotate the saw and lock the carriage at the required height.
5. Restore power, start dust collection, and let the blade reach full speed. Pull the carriage downward for vertical cuts. For horizontal cuts, feed the panel in the marked arrow direction. Move steadily without forcing; keep hands clear of the carriage and blade path.
6. If it binds, stop. Switch off and wait until the blade stops before clearing the problem.
7. Switch off and wait for a complete stop. Remove the pieces, return the carriage to the top, lock it, and clean up.
